# Your task: v481, the Swift modifier reworked, and the Giant modifier reviewed (branch `swift-rework`)
Simon: "fix all elite modifiers, the waves behind swift should actually sway in the correct direction and sway more realistically".

## What exists
- **The ribbons:** `ELD.swift` adds red ribbons (`RIBBON_G`, a 0.045 x 0.34 plane hanging down) on pivots at the back of the body. `ELA.swift` rotates each pivot rigidly: `rotation.x=-1.15-(moving?.25:0)+sin*.2`.
  - That rotation swings a downward-hanging ribbon toward the model's FRONT, while the pivots sit at its back, so the ribbons point the wrong way. Check the model's forward axis yourself and confirm.
  - The sway is a rigid flap, not cloth.
- **The dust:** `ELP.swift` emits dust with random velocity, not kicked backward.
- **Per class:** there are per-class pivot points (humanoid, horse, siege, bird, beast) and a red headband on humanoids.

## Build
1. **Ribbons that trail behind and move like cloth.** Each ribbon is a strip of a few segments whose vertices follow a simple verlet or spring chain.
   - Drive the chain by the foe's real world velocity (direction and speed from its frame-to-frame motion, including turns at road corners), gravity and a light flutter. The flutter is a wave travelling from root to tip, faster at speed.
   - Fast running streams them back and slightly up; slowed, stunned or stopped lets them hang and settle; a turn swings them outward.
   - It must also look right in the Bestiary: idle hang with a gentle breeze, rotating with the model.
   - Do it efficiently: few vertices, shared index, update in place, no allocations.
2. **Fit every unit that can be Swift.** Fit peasant, soldier, rider (horse and rider), raven, ram and wolf, and the bosses: ogre, kikimora, the dragons and wyrms, golems, beast, arachne, colossus, dreadk, voidk, ophanim, seraph and wyrmf. Each needs attachment points that make sense for it and move with it: a rider's sash and the horse's mane streamers, raven tail streamers, ram pennants, big streamers on dragons, and so on.
3. **Speed reads in 3D, subtle and never in the way.** Dust kicked backward opposite to the motion at the feet (sized per unit; none for flyers). Optionally faint wind-streak arcs or short after-images, at low opacity.
4. **Review the Giant modifier for bugs and looks.** It currently scales the model (1.5, x1.12 width) and multiplies health, `hitY` and lives. Check the health bar height, the selection and hit area, the shadow blob, the marker ring scale, the Bestiary scale, and that it stays correct on every unit and arena. Add a subtle 3D weight to it, such as heavy footfalls with small dust puffs or a slight ground shake decal, cheaply.
5. **Tests:** `tests/swx-test.js`.
   - The ribbons point opposite to the motion: measure the tip's world position against the velocity, over several headings including a turn.
   - They hang when the foe is stopped, every unit dresses without errors, there is no per-frame allocation growth (rough check), and cleanup works.
   - Cover the Giant fixes.
6. **Sheets:** before and after for each unit class at zoom 1 and close, a running foe in profile, the Bestiary, and the Giant.
