# Your task: v483, a bespoke Armoured design for every boss that can be Armoured (branch `armour-bosses`)
Simon: "armoured should not just be a template but rather a seperate design for every unit that can be armored".

## What exists
- **Who gets a modifier:** from wave 100 a boss can carry ONE modifier, 50% of the time, never on milestone bosses (`buildWave`, `B.milestone`; `MSB_T`).
- **The templates:** `ELD.armored` calls `ARMOR_D[type](C)` if present, else `armorClass(C)`.
  - Bosses get class templates: beast plates for dragons, wyrms and arachne, or the humanoid set, measured from the body bounding box. The ogre has a crude set.
  - Many bosses have no EBODY entry and get the soldier humanoid template on a non-humanoid body, which is wrong.
- **Your bosses:** ogre (variants Brute, Chieftain and Scarred Ogre), kikimora, dragon, dragon2, dragon2a, dragon3, dragon3a, wyrm, golem, golem2, beast, arachne, colossus, dreadk, voidk, wyrm2, wyrm2a, ophanim, seraph and wyrmf.
  - Find each model's builder: `enemyModel`/`unitModel` and the boss builders, with their animated parts (`m.wings`, `m.tail`, `m.jaw`, `m.heads`, `m.limbs`, `m.spin`, `e.tick`...).
  - dragon2a, dragon3a and wyrm2a are already "armoured" by design. Their Armoured modifier must still add something clearly distinct, e.g. heavier or gilded plating over the existing plates.

## Build
1. **One `ARMOR_D.<boss>` per boss** that reads as "this boss, heavily armoured", in its own style:
   - dragons: scale-plate barding on the neck and chest, wing-arm guards, a horned helm plate, a tail-spike sheath;
   - golems: iron bands and riveted rune plates bolted into the stone;
   - arachne: chitin-capping plates on the abdomen and leg greaves;
   - ophanim and seraph: gilded plate rings and feather-plates;
   - colossus, dreadk and voidk: heavier, darker plate in their own style;
   - kikimora: bark and bone armour; beast: plates between its spines.
2. **Attach every piece to the right moving part** (a wing bone, a neck segment, a jaw, a leg), so it moves with the animation and never floats or clips. Measure the real meshes and their bounding boxes per part.
3. **Performance.** Bosses are single, but some have many parts. Merge per part, cache geometry per boss type, and share materials. Respect the existing boss draw-call work (search `pfMergeGeo`).
4. **Tests:** `tests/arb-test.js`.
   - Each boss uses its own design, and the pieces stay attached across the animation.
   - Cover milestone bosses still never getting a modifier, cleanup, and no errors.
5. **Sheets:** every boss, plain against armoured, in the Bestiary (it can show elite looks with the elite button) and in game at zoom 1, plus close-ups of the moving parts mid-animation.
