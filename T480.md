# Your task: v480, five new enemy formations, plus a Formations page in the Bestiary (branch `formations-5`)
Simon asked to "add 5 more formations", and to "add some more features".

## What exists
- In `src/kingsroad-keep.html` (search `/* ================= Formations`): `FORMS` holds 4 formations: Shield Wall (from wave 3), Royal Escort (12), Rider Charge (5) and Siege Column (25).
- The other pieces are `formLayout(id,w)` (`[type, offset, distance, role]` rows), `rollFormation`, `spawnFormation` (role props such as the pavise and the banner, shared speed, the toast) and `formDR` (the shield wall's damage cut).
- `buildWave` inserts formations from wave 6 (35%, or 55% from wave 25, plus a second at 30% from wave 30). Formations take elites through `rollElite`.
- Dev helpers: `__kk.form(id,w)` and `__kk.formStats(w,n)`. Existing tests that touch formations: `grep -ln "spawnFormation\|FORMS" tests/*.js`.

## Build
1. **Five new formations.** Each needs its own clear mechanic with counterplay, a layout, role gear made by hand in 3D, a toast, and a minimum wave. They must work with elites, every arena and the overworld (`OW.on`).
   - **Peasant Mob** (from about wave 4): 9-10 peasants with torches and pitchforks in a loose crowd. They grow frenzied as they fall: each member slain makes the rest a few % faster (capped).
   - **Raven Murder** (about wave 8): 7 ravens flying in a V behind a lead raven. While the lead lives the flock keeps the V and something readable, e.g. it shares hits; when the lead falls the flock scatters.
   - **Pike Phalanx** (about wave 18): a tight block of soldiers with long pikes. While enough of them stand, the block cannot be pushed back and resists slows; it breaks below a threshold.
   - **Holy Procession** (about wave 16): monks carrying a reliquary or palanquin, with chanting light. Foes near the reliquary heal slowly; the reliquary bearer is the key target.
   - **Knight's Wedge** (about wave 22): knights in an arrowhead. The tip takes much less damage while the wedge holds; when the tip falls, the rest charge for a few seconds.

   You may refine the numbers and details. Keep five distinct, readable formations that fit the medieval setting and the existing ones.
2. **Balance.** Keep the overall formation rate per wave about the same as today, so the old four now come less often (measure with formStats before and after). Keep each formation's power in line with the existing ones (gold and HP per member as normal foes). Report the numbers.
3. **Feature: a Formations page in the Bestiary.** List all nine formations, each with a small 3D diorama of its layout (real unit models, slowly turning or idle), its name, the wave it comes from, and what it does. Match the Bestiary's style. The milestone bosses already have their own page (search `v300: the milestone bosses in the Bestiary`): follow that pattern for the tab or button.
4. **Tests:** `tests/fm5-test.js`. Cover each new layout and role, each mechanic with exact numbers, the formation rates, elites inside formations, cleanup, the Bestiary page opening and closing with nothing left behind, and no console errors.
5. **Sheets:** each new formation in game at zoom 1 and close, its mechanic moment (the scatter, the charge, the heal), and the Formations page.
