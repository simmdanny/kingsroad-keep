# Your task: v494, foes, elites, effects and particles at scale (branch `perf-crowd`)
Area: everything that comes and goes in the fight: foes (models, elite gear, markers, rings, health bars, shadow blobs), projectiles, particles (`emit`, `emitS`, sprays, puffs), the effects list (`fx`, `addFx`) and the JS hot loops (`update`, `moveEnemy`, `statusTick`, `towerUpdate`, `inRange`, `hurt`, healer loops, auras).

## Baseline
In the late scenario, about 160 foes make about 497 draw calls (about 3 per foe), and the max frame had 2173 draws. The game's JS is 2.7 ms update and p99 9.2 ms. Run the crowd scenario (160 foes, x40 HP) yourself as your main yardstick.

## Goals
1. **Foe draw calls:** under about 1.2 per foe.
   - Ideas, measure them: instance the repeated simple pieces (blob shadows, elite markers, gem rings, sparkles); merge each foe's still parts into one vertex-coloured mesh per variant; instance whole troop types where the animation allows.
   - Keep the elite looks (v479-v485 modules: ELD, ELA, ELP, ARMOR_D, the swift ribbons, regen looks, shield bubbles, markers), Invisible cloaks, the land dressings, hit flashes, death effects and the Bestiary working.
2. **Particles and effects:** one draw per particle system, pools, no per-frame allocations in emit or the effects loop.
3. **JS:** profile the crowd scenario and remove the hot spots.
   - Typical suspects: O(n^2) loops over all foes (healers, auras, splash), sorts every frame, `filter`/`map` allocations each step (`enemies=enemies.filter(...)` twice per update), `Math.hypot` in tight loops, closures per frame.
   - Aim for p99 under about 4 ms with 160 foes and the exact same game results: the same kills, damage and gold for a seeded fight. Prove that with a determinism test.
4. **Test:** `tests/pcr-test.js`, covering draw calls per foe with elites, the same results for a seeded fight before and after (record a fingerprint of kills, gold and damage), no allocation growth, and cleanup.
