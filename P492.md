# Your task: v492, the static world and the towers (branch `perf-world`)
Area: the board, terrain, scenery and props, the keep, the towers and the hero's still parts, and shadow casting. The v446 module (`pfBatch`, `pfFreeze`, `pfProps`, `pfLoose`, `pfTower`, `pfHeroFreeze`) is yours to extend.

## Baseline (perf-base, late scenario, wave 120, 20 towers, about 160 foes)
- Draw calls average about 1000, and up to 2173.
- The breakdown at the start of measurement: terrain 222, towers 249 (about 12 per tower), hero 13, keep 6. 334 objects cast shadows (terrain 160, towers 158), so the shadow pass draws most of them again.

## Goals
1. **Terrain:** about 220 draws down to under about 40 per land.
   - Merge the board and its scenery by material into few chunks, or convert colours to vertex colours so many materials become one.
   - Keep everything that animates, is clicked, is removed or is recoloured working (the land shaders, the Infinite Void dissolve, scorched trees, chests, landmarks, lore props).
2. **Towers:** about 12 down to 4 or fewer draws per tower.
   - Merge each tower's still body into one vertex-coloured mesh where the materials differ only in colour.
   - Keep the parts the game moves or swaps: heads, spinners, beacons, mastery flags, upgrades, the selection and hit rays, and the hero rig.
3. **Shadows:** cut the shadow casters by at least half without changing the look.
   - Small props and flat things don't need to cast, or should cast from merged meshes.
   - Pixel-compare the shadows, and consider whether the static part of the shadow map can be cached.
4. Prove the same picture in every land (pixel diffs) and that clicks, selling, upgrading, moving towers and land effects still work.
5. **Test:** `tests/pfw-test.js`, with the draw calls per land and per tower, pixel equality, interactions and memory freed.
