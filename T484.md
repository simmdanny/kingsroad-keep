# Your task: v484, Regenerating reworked with several variants, the Shielded bubble reworked, and 3D elite markers (branch `regen-shield`)
Simon: "fix all elite modifiers ... regenerating should have more variants and add some more features".

## What exists
- **Regenerating:** `ELD.regen` puts 6 to 8 moss clusters with glowing green buds round the body; `ELP.regen` emits green motes.
  - The mechanic is in `update`: `if(e.regen)e.hp=Math.min(e.maxHp,e.hp+e.maxHp*e.regen*dt)`, with `en.regen=.025` (2.5% of max HP a second).
  - `artStrip` can strip it (an artifact).
  - The Starfall world event also gives regen (`sfActive`).
- **Shielded:** `ELD.shielded` is an icosahedron bubble with edge lines that turns (`ELA.shielded`). `hurt` removes it when the shield breaks, with a `burst`.
- **The marker:** `eliteRing(a,i)` is a flat RingGeometry in the modifier's colour, under the foe. Other code touches `en.aura` and `en.aura2`: their `.visible`, `.scale` and `.material.opacity`. Keep those working.

## Build
1. **Regenerating variants.** At least 4 distinct looks, chosen per foe (deterministic from its spawn), each fitted to the body and moving with it, with each build on every unit type (troops and bosses). Suggested:
   - (a) **Verdant:** moss, sprouting leaves and buds that visibly grow while it heals.
   - (b) **Troll-blood:** wounds that glow and knit shut, with faint steam.
   - (c) **Blessed:** a slowly turning ring of small runes and golden motes.
   - (d) **Blood-leech:** dark veins pulsing with a heartbeat glow.
   - (e) **Fungal:** glowing caps and spore puffs.

   Name them, and show the variant name in the Bestiary elite label, e.g. "Elite: Regenerating (Troll-blood)".
2. **Regen features**, so the modifier plays better and reads clearly. Keep them modest and report the numbers.
   - The healing shows when it happens: a soft pulse along the body, scaled to the actual heal.
   - Counterplay: fire damage (and any burn) stops regeneration for 2 s, with a visible "wilting" or "searing" of the regen look.
   - Check the Starfall regen and any other source still work.
   - A short line in the Bestiary or help text explains the counterplay. Find where modifiers are explained (search `ELITE_N` and the Bestiary label) and add it there.
3. **Shielded reworked.** A better 3D bubble:
   - fitted per unit (size and centre from the real model);
   - a subtle hex-cell shimmer;
   - a ripple where a hit lands (from the hit direction);
   - a brief crack-up and shatter into real 3D shards when it breaks.

   Keep it see-through and never obstructive. Check the existing break paths (`hurt` and the line near 18482 that zeroes shields) still clean up.
4. **3D elite markers.** Replace the flat ring with a small, low 3D marker per modifier: a thin raised ring or bevelled band with a soft glow, in the modifier's colour, with a subtle motif per modifier. Keep it cheap (shared geometry and materials) and compatible with the `en.aura`/`en.aura2` uses (`visible`, `scale`, `material.opacity`). A second modifier stacks neatly.
5. **Tests:** `tests/rgx-test.js`.
   - The variants are chosen and dressed on every unit.
   - The heal amount is unchanged, and fire and burn suppress it for 2 s with exact numbers.
   - The shield ripple and shatter clean up, the markers keep the `aura` API, and there are no errors.
6. **Sheets:** each regen variant on several units, the heal pulse and the fire suppression, the shield's hit and shatter frames, and the markers at zoom 1 with a crowd.
