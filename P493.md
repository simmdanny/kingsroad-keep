# Your task: v493, shaders, materials and first-cast hitches (branch `perf-shaders`)
Area: shader program compilation, material and texture creation and sharing, and stutters (frame spikes).

## Baseline
In the late scenario, 10 shader programs compile during the measured fight, after warm-up. In the CPU profile, `getProgramInfoLog` and `getShaderInfoLog` were about 27% of all time: three.js compiling and checking shaders mid-battle. JS spikes reach a 17-25 ms max.

## Goals
1. **No shader compiles during play.**
   - Find which materials compile late: new materials per cast or per foe, `onBeforeCompile` variants, defines or lights changing.
   - Make them share programs: cache materials, stable `customProgramCacheKey`.
   - Pre-warm the rest. At load, or in idle time after load, compile every spell, effect, elite and land material variant (`renderer.compile` on a hidden scene, spread over idle frames so loading does not stall).
   - Turn off three.js's synchronous shader error checks (`renderer.debug.checkShaderErrors=false`) outside developer mode.
2. **First-cast hitches:** measure the frame time of the first cast of every scroll (Common to Secret) and of the first appearance of every elite and land effect, against a later one. Bring the first-cast spikes down to near the later ones.
3. **Materials and textures per cast:** find spells and effects that build new materials or canvas textures every cast and share them, or pool and reuse them. Keep artifact-set colour contexts working (`tests/smsets-test.js`, `tests/sdpx-test.js`) and leave no leaks (`renderer.info.memory`).
4. **GC spikes:** use the bench's `--profile` allocation report to find the biggest per-frame allocators in effects and spells, and remove them.
5. **Test:** `tests/psh-test.js`, covering no programs compiled during a scripted fight with casts of many scrolls, the first-cast spike bound, programs reused and nothing leaked.
