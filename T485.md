# Your task: v485, three new elite modifiers, and the Invisible modifier reviewed (branch `new-modifiers`)
Simon asked to "fix all elite modifiers" and to "add some more features".

## What exists
- **The six modifiers:** `AFFIX`, the colours, holds armored, swift, regen, shielded, giant and invisible (invisible is added later; search `AFFIX.invisible`). `ELITE_N` holds the names.
- **Rolling:** `rollElite(w,type)` (two modifiers from wave 35 at 25%; knights and monks never Armoured). The invisible-replacement wrappers use `Object.keys(AFFIX)`. Bosses get at most one modifier from wave 100, never milestone bosses.
- **The rest:** the Bestiary cycle is `ELITE_CYCLE` and `galElite`. `artStrip` strips one modifier. Looks go through `ELD`/`ELA`/`ELP` (v479).

## Build
1. **Three new modifiers**, each with a clear mechanic and counterplay, a bespoke 3D look fitted to each unit class (via `ELD`/`ELA`/`ELP`), a marker colour, a Bestiary entry, an `artStrip` branch, and a minimum wave.
   - **Splitting** (troops only, never bosses; from about wave 15): a glowing seam runs down the body. On death the foe tears in two with a short 3D effect: two smaller copies, at about 0.7 scale and about 30% max HP each, with no modifiers, no gold beyond a small amount, and no lives beyond 1 each. They carry on from where it fell.
   - **Blinking** (from about wave 25): every few seconds it blinks a short way forward along the road (about 1 to 1.5 tiles). It leaves a fading after-image and a small 3D implosion and arrival burst, and is untargetable for that instant only. Stuns, roots and holds stop the blink.
   - **Warded** (from about wave 40): it resists the element of the last element hit it took by 40%. A small orbiting 3D rune shows that element's colour, and the ward shifts with a visible flash when a new element lands. Physical damage is never warded.
   - Make the rolls respect the rules: bosses one modifier, no Splitting on bosses, knight and monk rules. Keep the overall elite rate unchanged.
2. **Wire them everywhere.** Check every use of `AFFIX`, `ELITE_N`, `rollElite`, `ELITE_CYCLE`, `artStrip`, `e.elites` and `e.elite` (`grep -n`), every arena and the overworld, saves and resume (`serializeRun`: do elites survive a resume?), and the dev panel if it lists modifiers.
3. **Review the Invisible modifier** for bugs and looks on every unit (`invisLook`, reveal) and fix what is wrong.
4. **Tests:** `tests/nmx-test.js`.
   - Each mechanic with exact numbers: the split counts, HP and scale; the blink distance, cadence and the hold stopping it; the ward percentage and its switching.
   - Roll rules and rates, `artStrip`, the Bestiary cycle, cleanup, no errors.
5. **Sheets:** each new modifier on several units in the Bestiary and in game at zoom 1, with the split, blink and ward-shift moments, and the Invisible review.
